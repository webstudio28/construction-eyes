# Construction Eyes: image selection

Selected 2026-09-23. 20 curated photographs.

Open [the visual catalog](catalog.html) to review all files. Machine-readable metadata and page mappings are in [image-manifest.json](image-manifest.json).

## Scope

Images are saved under `src/assets/images/{heroes,services,sections,projects}` as appropriate. Existing assets, page templates and live image references are untouched. There is one local WebP per selected photograph; placements reuse these files instead of storing duplicate copies.

All photos were visually inspected. Files preserve composition and color; only proportional sizing and WebP compression were applied. Hero images have a maximum edge of 2400 px; other images 2000 px. Portrait assets retain their full proportions; see crop advice rather than blindly center-cropping every image.

## Use and provenance

These images illustrate services. They are not evidence of company staff, customer relationships, fleet ownership, equipment inventory or completed jobs. Pexels/Unsplash source and license links are listed per image. The mobile tower from Wikimedia Commons requires author credit, a source/license link and identification of the resize/WebP changes. Its derivative remains CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Project-gallery integration

The ten named Bulgarian projects currently contain claims such as “Снимки от обекта” and “Реални кадри от изпълнението и крайния резултат.” The supplied stock photos must not be presented as those real projects. If these stock illustrations are connected, use “Илюстративна галерия” and “Снимките илюстрират типа скеле и не са от конкретния обект.”, and use descriptive image alt text from the manifest instead of the named project as photo alt text. Client originals remain the proper final portfolio material.

Video cover choices are topic illustrations, not verified frames. Some existing project video links use a placeholder YouTube ID; video content cleanup is outside this asset-collection pass.
